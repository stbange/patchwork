## Why

A scrap quilt often uses more than five fabrics. Hexagons meet in threes, clamshells nest along curves, and a single appliqué stem can travel across several seams. A rectangular block library alone cannot describe those sewing decisions.

## What Changes

- Grow the fabric basket from five starter swatches to 26 editable fabrics.
- Preview, paint and combine continuous piecing and quilt-wide appliqué. Export the actual fabric pieces, including full-size tracing pages.
- Preserve existing block layouts, named palettes, languages, units and saved plans.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `quilt-planning` covers a larger fabric basket, quilt-wide piecing and layered appliqué.

## Impact

The shared C model, SDL design wall, versioned save format, exports and native/browser journeys change together.
