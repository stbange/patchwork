## Why

Quilters need to read the planner in their own language, work in their preferred units, compare fabrics, and combine motifs with different seam layouts. A printable PDF lets them take the quilt map and cutting instructions to the sewing table.

## What Changes

- Localize the planner and cutting instructions in English, German and Polish.
- Let quilters enter block sizes in centimetres or inches and remember their preferred units.
- Add connected motifs, staggered seams, and block-by-block placement of different patterns.
- Offer 100 named fabric palettes and custom RGB colors for every swatch.
- Download or save PDF cutting sheets with the quilt map and measurements.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `quilt-planning` covers mixed blocks, color, localized measures and PDF export.

### New Capabilities

- `planner-localization` covers language selection, accessibility text and exported instructions.

## Impact

The shared C and SDL app changes on Windows, Linux and Emscripten. Existing square and single-pattern files remain readable. GitHub Pages publishes the tested browser build.
