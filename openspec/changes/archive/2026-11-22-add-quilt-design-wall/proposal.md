## Why

A quilter can spend an afternoon arranging fabric on the table and lose the layout when she clears it. A digital design wall lets her try another color, come back tomorrow, and take an exact list of pieces to the cutting mat.

## What Changes

- Open on an editable Garden Path table topper. Pick a labeled fabric swatch, then touch or click a square to replace it. Undo an experiment in one step.
- Change the number of rows and columns and the finished size of a square. See what the quilt top will measure after sewing.
- Switch to a cutting sheet with a lettered row map, piece counts and a seam allowance already added. Take the sheet to the sewing table.
- Remember one current quilt on this device. Use the same design on Windows, Linux and the web.

## Impact

The first build introduces a C application, SDL graphics, a local project format and automated checks. The editable two-dimensional wall is the starting point. A later read-only 3D preview could show the same design draped over a table or bed once a quilter has tried the basic workflow.
